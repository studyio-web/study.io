# Premium Collectible Avatar Prompts (Pro Exclusive)

This document contains the prompt guidelines and 12 ready-to-use image-generation prompts for the 12 elite collectible avatars in study.io.

---

## Shared Master Style Paragraph

> **Style Standard:**
> A masterfully painted, glossy, dark-fantasy head-and-shoulders portrait. Soft cinematic key lighting from the upper left highlights rich facial contours and reflective materials, while a vibrant colored rim light from the right casts a sharp edge glow in the character's signature color. The backdrop is a smooth, atmospheric dark vertical gradient in the signature tint, dusted with subtle floating luminescent ember particles and ethereal bokeh. The character's face and primary silhouette are strictly centered within the central 80% safe zone so circular crops never clip features. Highly detailed textures, expressive gaze, diverse ethnicities and features, ornate glowing accessories, no text, no watermarks, no logos, perfectly clean framing.
